Frequently asked questions

Clarity for your
next step.

Where should we start with automation?

Start with high-impact business workflows and repetitive scenarios with stable rules. An assessment helps set priorities and avoid unnecessary maintenance.

Can tests be integrated into CI/CD?

Yes. The strategy can include pipeline execution, published reports and approval criteria suited to the duration and purpose of each test suite.

How do we measure results?

Combine metrics such as critical journey coverage, execution time, test stability and defects found before production. Targets depend on your starting point and scope.
